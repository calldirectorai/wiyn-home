Every line and device in your office, mapped

The Network Assessment is a full inventory of your phone lines, phone system, router and switches. You find out what is still supported, what could stop the office, and what to fix first.

What you get

Every phone line, and what is on it

Main numbers, fax, alarm, elevator, card terminal and door-entry lines, matched to what you are billed for.

The age of your equipment

Your phone system, router and switches, with whether the maker still supports each one.

Where one failure stops the office

The single devices or lines that everything else depends on.

What you can stop paying for

Lines and services that no longer do anything for you.

What to upgrade, in order

A short written report with the changes ranked, so you can do them at your own pace.

Who it is for

Offices that got an "Aging" or "End of life" grade on the free check, offices with copper phone lines, and any owner who is not sure what every line on the phone bill is for.

Alarm, elevator and door-entry lines are listed in the report, and flagged for a specialist review before any change.

Common questions

No. The report is yours to keep and use however you like.

Yes, if you have not. It takes about two minutes and tells you whether an assessment is worth it. Start the free check.
