Common questions

Do I need to know technical details?

No. Every question has a "Not sure" answer. Not knowing is useful information too.

Will you try to sell me a whole new system?

No. Most offices keep the phone number and phone service they have. What we usually recommend first is making sure every call gets answered.

What about alarm, elevator and door-entry lines?

Those lines need a specialist review before any change. We flag them and never assume a standard replacement fits.

Why does the age of my phone lines matter now?

Phone companies are retiring copper phone lines. Verizon has asked the FCC to end copper phone service in parts of Maryland and DC as early as November 30, 2026, pending approval. Not every line changes that day, but it is better to know what you have first.
